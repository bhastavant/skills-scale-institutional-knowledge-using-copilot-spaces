# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, align teams on implementation decisions, and help translate product goals into a feasible technical plan.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architecture trade-offs
- Help break strategic initiatives into delivery-ready work
- Support team-level planning, sequencing, and dependency management
- Coordinate technical design reviews and implementation standards
- Identify delivery risks and align engineering with product timelines

### Goals
- Maintain implementation quality and technical consistency
- Reduce unnecessary rework and integration risk
- Ensure engineering work remains aligned with product outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and engineering planning sessions
- Cross-team dependency reviews and technical risk updates
- Collaboration with PMs, Product Managers, and Developers on feasibility and sequencing

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to turn roadmap priorities into technical plans
- Partners with Project Managers to surface dependencies, staffing constraints, and milestone risks
- Guides Developers on technical decisions, quality standards, and implementation trade-offs

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define quality expectations, validate that work meets acceptance criteria, and help the team understand release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage approach, and quality gates
- Review acceptance criteria and ensure they are testable
- Coordinate functional, regression, and release validation activities
- Surface defects, quality trends, and release readiness risks
- Support manual QA and stakeholder signoff when needed

### Goals
- Deliver reliable and user-friendly outcomes
- Reduce escaped defects and rework in production
- Improve confidence in release quality and acceptance criteria coverage

### Typical Communication
- Test planning, QA status reviews, and release readiness meetings
- Defect triage, validation status, and signoff updates
- Collaboration with Developers, Product Managers, and Release Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm that acceptance criteria are measurable and user-focused
- Works with Developers to validate fixes, regressions, and quality risks
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Release Managers on release readiness checkpoints and deployment confidence

---

## Security / Compliance Representatives

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Representatives ensure projects meet governance, risk, and secure-delivery requirements across the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review project changes for security, privacy, and compliance implications
- Identify risks in architecture, data handling, access controls, and third-party integrations
- Define required controls and approval checkpoints for sensitive work
- Support incident readiness and response coordination when security concerns arise
- Help teams align with policy and regulatory obligations

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Build trust in the system and delivery process
- Ensure controls are integrated early rather than added late

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, policy checks, and risk escalation discussions
- Compliance or governance updates during planning and release readiness
- Coordination with Engineering Leads, PMs, and Sponsors on material risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk, dependencies, and approval needs
- Partners with Engineering Leads and Developers to review secure design and implementation choices
- Escalates high-risk items to Sponsors or leadership when necessary

---

## Release Managers / Deployment Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment planning, communication, and rollout readiness to reduce operational risk during change release.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule release windows and communicate deployment plans
- Coordinate pre-release checks, smoke tests, and rollback readiness
- Align stakeholders on production changes, risks, and announcement timing
- Support post-deploy verification and issue escalation
- Maintain key deployment documentation and lessons learned

### Goals
- Reduce deployment failures and service disruption
- Improve communication during production changes
- Create repeatable, low-risk release practices

### Typical Communication
- Release planning, go/no-go reviews, and post-deploy check-ins
- Status updates with engineering, support, and leadership teams
- Coordination with QA and Project Managers for release readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Leads and Developers to confirm deployment readiness and rollback plans
- Aligns with Project Managers on timelines, stakeholder communication, and risk tracking
- Coordinates with Product Managers on business-impacting release messaging and support readiness

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons connect product delivery to end-user impact, feedback, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, support trends, and adoption issues
- Help prioritize user pain points and success metrics
- Coordinate communication between customers, support teams, and delivery teams
- Align release readiness with service and support readiness
- Help identify operational issues that may require follow-up work

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and satisfaction
- Reduce friction between delivery teams and support operations
- Ensure product changes match user needs and support realities

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback loops, support escalation reviews, and readiness check-ins
- Collaboration with Product Managers, PMs, and release teams
- Feedback synthesis for backlog prioritization and retrospective planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Shares user impact insights with Product Managers to inform prioritization
- Escalates operational concerns to Project Managers and Engineering Leads
- Supports release communication and post-launch monitoring with product and support stakeholders

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners ensure the team can measure outcomes, monitor changes, and use evidence to guide iteration.

### Responsibilities
- Define key metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Validate that success metrics are measurable and actionable
- Help assess product impact before and after launch
- Support analysis of trends, usage, quality, and performance signals
- Identify gaps in instrumentation and analytics readiness

### Goals
- Improve decision quality through data-informed iteration
- Ensure teams can measure whether business outcomes are being achieved
- Make success metrics visible and actionable across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions, dashboard reviews, and outcome reporting
- Collaboration with Product Managers and stakeholders on business value tracking
- Coordination with Engineering leads and QA on monitoring and validation coverage

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to align metrics with business goals
- Coordinates with Developers and Engineering Leads on instrumentation and data quality
- Provides insight to Project Managers and Sponsors during milestone and release reviews

---

## Sponsors / Executive Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Executive Stakeholders provide strategic direction, funding, and escalation support for higher-impact initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business sponsorship and prioritization context
- Confirm strategic alignment and funding for project work
- Support cross-functional decision-making and escalations when needed
- Review milestone progress, risks, and major trade-offs
- Sponsor problem resolution when project outcomes are at risk

### Goals
- Ensure investment is aligned with strategic priorities
- Support decisions that reduce risk and improve delivery confidence
- Maintain visibility into outcomes and business impact

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates, steering committee reviews, and strategic risk escalation
- Direction setting during kickoff, milestones, and major decision points
- Coordination with Product Leads, PMs, and Project Sponsors

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receives updates from Project Managers and Product Managers on outcomes, risks, and decisions
- Supports engagement and escalation when dependencies or barriers require leadership action
- Helps balance business priorities with delivery constraints and resource needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The roles above complement the existing product, project, and engineering personas by making accountability, dependencies, and communication paths more explicit across the full delivery lifecycle.
