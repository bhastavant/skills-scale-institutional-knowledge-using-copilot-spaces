# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative project management lifecycle focused on customer value, clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous improvement:

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, assess resources and risks, and make a go/no-go decision.
2. **Planning** — Turn approved initiatives into prioritized, estimated backlogs with acceptance criteria, milestones, dependencies, a release plan, and a Definition of Done.
3. **Execution and tracking** — Coordinate delivery through project boards, standups, delivery syncs, reviews, quality checks, and progress metrics while escalating blockers through defined paths.
4. **Risk management and communication** — Maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, share regular status updates, and escalate issues to the appropriate leadership level.
5. **Release and deployment** — Verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, rollback plans, staging smoke tests, production deployment, and post-deployment communication.
6. **Retrospective and continuous improvement** — Capture what went well, identify improvements, assign follow-up actions, and measure their impact after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents.
